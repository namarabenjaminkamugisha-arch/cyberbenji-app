What it does

Practical protection that works without rooting your phone, signing up, or paying.

🔗

Scan links, files & messages

Check a URL, QR code, downloaded file or suspicious message against known-threat intelligence before you trust it.

🛡️

Live threat monitor

Watches incoming notifications for phishing and scam patterns and warns you the moment something risky arrives.

🌐

DNS Guard

Blocks ads, trackers, malware and adult domains device-wide using a local, private DNS filter — no traffic leaves your phone unprotected.

Digital footprint (OSINT)

Privacy tools

Install in three steps

CyberBenji is distributed directly as an APK, so you install it yourself rather than through a store.

Download the APK

Tap “Download for Android” above on your phone.

Allow the install

When prompted, allow your browser or Files app to “install unknown apps”. This is required for any app installed outside the Play Store.

Open CyberBenji

Tap the downloaded file to install, then open it. No account or payment — you’re ready.

Download won’t finish?

Some browsers (especially Chrome) block .apk files — the download reaches 100% then stops or disappears. Two free ways around it:

Option A — download the ZIP

Grab the zipped version, then open it in your Files app → extract → tap the .apk inside to install. Browsers don’t block ZIP files.

⬇️ Download CyberBenji (ZIP, ~16 MB)

Option B — use a different browser

Open this page in Firefox (or Samsung Internet) and tap Download — they let .apk files finish downloading normally. Then tap the file to install.

After it downloads, Android may say the app is “unknown” — tap More details → Install anyway. That’s normal for any app installed outside the Play Store.

Privacy & security

Your data stays on your phone

Scans and checks run on-device. CyberBenji has no user accounts and no analytics tracking you.

When a feature needs external threat intelligence (for example to check a link), only the item being checked is sent to the relevant lookup service — never your personal data.

Honest about limits

CyberBenji is a real toolkit, not magic. It raises the bar against common threats but can’t guarantee protection from every attack.

It’s Android-only for now. Some features rely on Android system services and wouldn’t work the same way on other platforms.
